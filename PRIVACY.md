# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-04-18

DiffMerge is a native macOS application that runs entirely on your Mac. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app does — and does not — handle.

## Summary

**DiffMerge does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal data.**

Everything you do in DiffMerge happens locally on your Mac.

## What we do not collect

- No personally identifiable information (PII)
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No file contents — files you open stay on your device
- No file metadata (names, paths, sizes, types)
- No crash reports sent to us
- No device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or location data
- No account creation or login
- No cookies, trackers, or third-party SDKs

## What the app does

- Reads files you explicitly open via the macOS file picker
- Writes files you explicitly save to locations you choose
- Computes and displays differences between the files you open
- Reads your Mac App Store purchase receipt (handled by Apple, not by us) to verify a purchase if applicable

## Sandboxing and network

DiffMerge is a sandboxed macOS application. It has access only to files you explicitly select through the system file picker. It cannot access other files on your system.

The app has **no network entitlement** and makes **no network requests**. It cannot connect to the internet, period.

## Apple and the Mac App Store

When you download DiffMerge from the Mac App Store, Apple may collect information as described in Apple's own [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We do not receive personal information about you from Apple. We can view anonymized, aggregated App Store Connect statistics such as download counts by country and app version.

## Children

DiffMerge is rated 4+ and contains no age-inappropriate content. Since we collect no personal data from anyone, we do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version of the app or of this document, the updated policy will be posted at this URL and the effective date above will be updated.
